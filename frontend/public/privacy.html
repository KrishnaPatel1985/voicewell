Privacy

Voicewell is a hackathon demo. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information.

What we keep

Nothing about you is saved on our side. While you use Voicewell, a small amount of session information is held in memory only and is deleted after 30 minutes of inactivity. Your age and your choices are never written to a database or a file.

Your voice

If you practise by voice, your audio is sent to Inworld to turn speech into text and to create the practice partner's replies, and to Modulate to notice the tone of your voice. They process it to give you a response, and Voicewell does not keep the audio or the transcript afterwards.

Age check and permissions

The age check and any grown-up approval are handled by k-ID. Features like AI chat and voice only turn on if your permissions allow them.

Not medical advice

Voicewell helps you practise conversations. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Please talk to a doctor or a trusted adult about any health concern.
