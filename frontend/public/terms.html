Terms

Voicewell is a hackathon demo, offered as is for practising conversations.

Using Voicewell

Use it kindly and for practice only. The practice partner is an AI role-play, not a real person, a doctor or a counsellor.

Not medical advice

Nothing in Voicewell is a diagnosis, treatment or medical advice. If you are worried about your health, talk to a doctor or a trusted adult. If you are in danger now, contact your local emergency number.

Your data

Nothing about you is stored. Audio is processed by Inworld and Modulate to give you a response and is then discarded. See the privacy page.

Demo only

Features may change or stop working at any time.
